THE PROBLEM

Sellers drown in products.
Generic AI doesn't know your taste.

Thousands of products launch daily. Big sellers hire buyers to sift them. Independent sellers scroll at midnight, alone. And every AI judge gives the same generic answer to everyone — because it never learned what you would buy.

THE INSIGHT

Don't triage 2,000 products.
Find the one with the gap.

The same leather tote: $12 to source locally, €45 in the EU.
That gap is a business — if you find it first.

Reflex starts from your top searches — your niche, your edge — and hunts the single best risk-adjusted arbitrage.
