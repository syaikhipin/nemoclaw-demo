The idea

Buy where it's cheap.
Sell where it's expensive.

Cross-border arbitrage is simple. Finding the one product worth working — before everyone else — is the hard part. Reflex does it in five steps:

01

Your top searches

What you keep looking up is your edge — the niche you understand. The agent hunts there, not in generic trend lists.

02

Margin-gap scan

For products in your space it compares local sourcing cost vs EU retail and does the margin math.

03

The one

Not fifty maybes — the single best risk-adjusted opportunity, picked by your distilled deal instinct.

04

Verify

The slow brain stress-tests it: competition, shipping, quality risk — before a cent is considered.

05

Work the deal

Finds suppliers, drafts outreach, rehearses the negotiation. Money never moves without you.

The novelty

Your deal criteria, distilled

Psychologist Daniel Kahneman showed humans think in two systems — fast instinct, slow analysis. Reflex is built the same way. The fast one is the breakthrough: a tiny model that is a compressed copy of your judgment. Like espresso from a pot of coffee — all the flavor, none of the volume.

🟢 System One🔵 System Two
WhatYour distilled deal instinctA large AI analyst (GLM / Hermes)
Speed~16 milliseconds~10–60 seconds
CostFree, runs on your laptopPaid API call
HandlesScreening thousands of candidatesVerifying the one opportunity

Every verdict is typed — shortlist / pass / research with a calibrated probability and a rationale — and the Noul safety gate sits in front of everything consequential: the agent can research, draft, and negotiate, but it can never move money without you. Reflex recommends; it never transacts.

reflex/noul.py — the safety gate
# Money movement / commitment — blocked.
_BLOCKED = re.compile(
    r"\b(buy|purchase|order|checkout|pay|payment|transfer|wire|deposit|"
    r"pre-?order|send money|charge (my|the) card|place .*order|"
    r"close the deal|sign .*contract|send .*contract)\b",
    re.IGNORECASE,
)

def check(action: str) -> NoulVerdict:
    """Return allowed=False for anything that moves money."""
    ...
    if _BLOCKED.search(action):
        return NoulVerdict(action=action, allowed=False, reason=(
            "Noul safety gate: this action would move money. "
            "Reflex drafts and negotiates; it never transacts "
            "without your approval."))
